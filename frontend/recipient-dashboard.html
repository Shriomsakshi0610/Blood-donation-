Emergency Blood Transfusion Protocols

Important Safety & Legal Information:

  • All voluntary donations must take place exclusively at registered hospital blood banks or certified blood collection centers.
  • Direct patient-to-donor cross-matching, antibody screening, and infectious disease testing are mandatory prior to any transfusion.
  • Never pay or transfer monetary compensation to any individual claiming to sell blood. Blood sale is illegal under the National Blood Policy.
  • Contact BloodConnect emergency helpline (1800-BLOOD-CONNECT) for any coordination support.
