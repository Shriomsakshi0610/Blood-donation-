Our Mission

Connecting Donors, Saving Lives

BloodConnect is a voluntary, non-profit digital blood donation platform designed to bridge the gap between voluntary donors and critical patients during medical emergencies.

🛡️

Zero Commercialization

We operate strictly under the National Blood Policy. Blood is a gift of life; our platform is 100% free, voluntary, and non-commercial.

⚡

Speed in Emergencies

When traumatic injuries or emergency surgeries occur, every second counts. Our proximity matcher alerts matching local donors within minutes.

🔒

Privacy & Security First

Exact donor addresses are never exposed publicly. Direct contact occurs only through authenticated, emergency-verified hospital channels.

Can I Donate Blood? — Eligibility Criteria

Standard medical guidelines recommended by the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC).

✓ Basic Requirements

  • Age Between 18 and 65 years old
  • Weight Minimum 45 kg (99 lbs)
  • Hemoglobin 12.5 g/dL or higher
  • Pulse 50 to 100 beats/minute, regular
  • Interval Minimum 90 days (3 months) since last whole blood donation

✕ Temporary Deferrals

  • Tattoo/Piercing Defer for 6 months after procedure
  • Dental Surgery Defer for 72 hours to 1 month
  • Antibiotics Defer until 7 days after course completion
  • Vaccinations Defer for 14-28 days depending on vaccine
  • Pregnancy Defer during pregnancy and 12 months post-delivery
